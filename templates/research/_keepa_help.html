How to get the Keepa file (no API needed)
  1. Sign in at keepa.com (a Data subscription is needed to export).
  2. Open the Product Viewer — Keepa’s bulk ASIN lookup (not “Product Finder”, which only filters a whole category).
  3. Paste the ASINs of the products in your niche (up to 10,000), or upload them as a CSV/text list. Easiest source: copy the ASIN column from the Xray Products export you’re uploading here — that already lists the market’s products and competitors.
  4. Choose these columns before exporting: ASIN, Title, Brand, Buy Box (Current / Lowest / Highest / 90-day avg), Sales Rank, Reviews Rating, Review Count, Bought in past month, Categories (Root/Sub/Tree), Color, Size, Variation ASINs, Package dimensions & weight, Features/Description.
  5. Click Export → CSV/XLSX and download the file.
  6. Upload it below. Rows are matched by ASIN; any product not in the file is marked “Unknown” (Rule 0). If the tool later needs variation ASINs that aren’t in your file, add them to the Product Viewer list and re-upload a combined export.

Tip: Product Viewer takes a specific ASIN list → precise coverage. Product Finder only filters a category by criteria (no ASIN/keyword box), so use Product Viewer for this step.
